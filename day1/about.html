About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for writing down short notes the moment an idea comes to you. It keeps everything in one place so you never lose a thought, a reminder or a to-do again.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the text box on the Home page.
  2. Click "Add Note" or press Enter to save it.
  3. Read your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers
  • Simple, clean layout with nothing distracting

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
